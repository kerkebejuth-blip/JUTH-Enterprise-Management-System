# System Vision

## Status

Version 1.0 Enterprise Architecture Baseline.

## Vision Statement

JUTH HOS shall become a world-class Enterprise Hospital Operating System that enables safe, efficient, interoperable, intelligent, and patient-centred healthcare delivery for Jos University Teaching Hospital and future healthcare networks.

## Platform Vision

The system shall provide a unified enterprise environment for clinical, operational, administrative, financial, research, education, and governance workflows.

It shall support the hospital as an integrated organization rather than a collection of disconnected departments.

## Experience Vision

The Staff Portal shall serve as the enterprise clinical workspace. Specialty modules shall extend the shared workspace and preserve patient context, consistent navigation, accessibility, and shared design system behavior.

The platform shall minimize unnecessary navigation, reduce cognitive load, and provide the right clinical information in the right context.

## Backend Vision

The backend shall model healthcare operations through Clean Architecture, Domain-Driven Design, a modular monolith deployment strategy, microservice-ready boundaries, REST-first APIs, and event-driven readiness.

The backend exists to model healthcare operations faithfully, not to expose CRUD endpoints over database tables.

## Data Vision

Data architecture shall preserve integrity, provenance, auditability, historical records, and future interoperability.

Data shall be governed through domain ownership, transaction boundaries, audit fields, versioning, migration discipline, and standards mapping.

## Security Vision

Security shall be built into the platform through identity, authentication, authorization, audit logging, secrets management, encryption readiness, least privilege, and fail-closed behavior.

## Interoperability Vision

The platform shall be ready for healthcare interoperability through REST, OpenAPI, FHIR, HL7, DICOM, terminology mapping, and event-driven integration patterns.

## AI Vision

AI shall be a governed platform capability. AI may assist development and future clinical workflows, but it shall not define architecture or replace authorized professional judgment.

## Enterprise Readiness Vision

JUTH HOS shall be considered enterprise-ready when architecture, security, testing, deployment, documentation, observability, clinical safety, and operational governance are all repository-resident and enforceable.
