# Clinical Rules

## Status

Version 1.0 Enterprise Architecture Baseline.

## Purpose

Clinical rules protect patient safety and clinical integrity.

## Governance

Clinical rules require:

- Domain Blueprint documentation.
- Clinical reviewer input where applicable.
- Security and audit consideration.
- Test strategy.
- Clear ownership.

## Implementation Rule

Clinical rules must not be inferred by AI or developers without approved domain evidence.

## Safety Areas

High-risk areas include patient identity, allergies, medications, orders, results, alerts, discharge, handover, and AI-assisted recommendations.
