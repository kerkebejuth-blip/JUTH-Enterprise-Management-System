## 1. PURPOSE

This volume defines how Architecture Decision Records govern architectural evolution in JUTH HOS.

## 2. ADR PURPOSE

ADRs capture significant architecture decisions, alternatives considered, consequences, and references.

They provide traceability for future teams and prevent undocumented architectural drift.

## 3. WHEN AN ADR IS REQUIRED

An ADR is required for changes affecting:

- Architecture style
- Repository structure
- Framework selection
- Module boundaries
- Data architecture
- Security model
- Integration pattern
- Deployment strategy
- AI integration principles
- Healthcare interoperability strategy

## 4. ADR STATUS

ADR status values include:

- Proposed
- Approved
- Superseded
- Deprecated
- Rejected

Only approved ADRs govern implementation.

## 5. ADR CONTENT

Each ADR shall include:

- Title
- Status
- Context
- Decision
- Alternatives
- Consequences
- References

## 6. GOVERNANCE

ADRs are reviewed by the Chief Software Architect and relevant domain, security, clinical, or operations reviewers.

Approved ADRs sit below the Constitution and above engineering standards in the decision hierarchy.

## 7. SUMMARY

ADRs provide controlled architectural evolution and preserve the reasoning behind major platform decisions.
