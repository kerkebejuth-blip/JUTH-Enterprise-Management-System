## 1. PURPOSE

This Constitution establishes the governing principles, architectural vision, engineering standards, development governance, and decision-making framework for the JUTH Enterprise Hospital Operating System (JUTH HOS).

It defines how the platform shall be designed, implemented, reviewed, evolved, and maintained throughout its lifecycle.

The Constitution is the highest technical authority within the project after explicit direction from the Project Owner.

All implementation activities—including software development, documentation, testing, DevOps, security engineering, UI/UX, database engineering, and AI-assisted development—shall comply with this Constitution.

## 2. VISION

To build a world-class Enterprise Hospital Operating System that enables safe, efficient, interoperable, intelligent, and patient-centred healthcare delivery.

The platform shall serve as the digital operating environment for Jos University Teaching Hospital while remaining architecturally capable of supporting multiple hospitals, healthcare networks, and future national deployments.

## 3. MISSION

The mission of JUTH HOS is to deliver an integrated enterprise healthcare platform that:

- Improves patient care.
- Supports clinicians.
- Enables operational excellence.
- Preserves complete longitudinal medical records.
- Promotes interoperability using international healthcare standards.
- Enables secure clinical collaboration.
- Supports education and research.
- Provides trustworthy auditability.
- Enables future AI-assisted clinical workflows.
