# ADR-009: Healthcare Standards (FHIR, HL7, DICOM)

## Status

Approved

## Context

JUTH HOS must be capable of integration with healthcare systems, diagnostic equipment, national health systems, and future interoperability networks.

## Decision

The platform shall design clinical and integration contracts with readiness for FHIR, HL7, DICOM, and controlled terminology mapping.

## Alternatives

- Proprietary-only integration contracts.
- Deferred interoperability planning.
- Department-specific integration formats.

## Consequences

- Domain blueprints must include FHIR mapping where applicable.
- Diagnostic domains must consider DICOM readiness.
- Integration adapters shall protect the domain model from external schema drift.
- Standards support may be implemented incrementally.
