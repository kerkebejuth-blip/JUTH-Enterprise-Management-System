# ADR-007: Modular Monolith Strategy

## Status

Approved

## Context

The platform requires strong modular boundaries but does not yet require the operational complexity of distributed microservices.

## Decision

JUTH HOS shall initially deploy as a modular monolith with microservice-ready boundaries.

## Alternatives

- Distributed microservices from the beginning.
- A tightly coupled monolith.
- Separate departmental applications.

## Consequences

- Modules communicate through explicit interfaces.
- Extraction to services remains possible when justified.
- Operational complexity stays manageable during foundation and early feature delivery.
- Module boundary violations must be treated as architecture issues.
