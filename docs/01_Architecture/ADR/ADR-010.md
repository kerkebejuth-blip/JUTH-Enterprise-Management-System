# ADR-010: AI Integration Principles

## Status

Approved

## Context

JUTH HOS will use AI-assisted engineering and may later support AI-assisted clinical workflows. These capabilities must not compromise safety, security, accountability, or architecture governance.

## Decision

AI shall be treated as a governed platform capability. AI tools may assist development and future clinical workflows, but they do not define architecture or replace authorized professional judgment.

## Alternatives

- Uncontrolled AI usage.
- Complete prohibition of AI tooling.
- External chatbot-only model for clinical assistance.

## Consequences

- AI-generated code requires human review and validation.
- AI-assisted clinical capabilities must be auditable, context-aware, non-blocking, and clinically governed.
- Prompts or outputs that change architecture require review.
- Patient data and secrets must not be exposed to unauthorized AI tools.
