# ADR-005: Clean Architecture

## Status

Approved

## Context

JUTH HOS must preserve clinical business rules independently from frameworks, databases, and user interface concerns.

## Decision

The platform shall follow Clean Architecture. Dependencies shall point inward from presentation and infrastructure toward application and domain logic.

## Alternatives

- Controller-centered business logic.
- Database-centered active record design.
- UI-driven workflow logic.

## Consequences

- Domain logic remains testable and framework-independent.
- Controllers coordinate requests but do not own business rules.
- Repositories abstract persistence concerns.
- Architectural review must reject dependency direction violations.
